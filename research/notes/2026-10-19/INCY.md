## What it does
Biotech that sells its own drugs. **Jakafi** (ruxolitinib, for blood cancers and graft-versus-host disease) is the core product, with about $3.2B of 2026 guidance out of $5.1-5.3B total sales. **Opzelura** (a ruxolitinib cream) and a growing oncology and immunology portfolio make up the rest.

## Why the screen likes it
It has very high gross margins, net cash (it holds more cash than debt), buybacks, a low forward P/E and strong recent sales momentum.

## What the screen can't see
- **The patent cliff.** Incyte's own filings list Jakafi's composition-of-matter patent expiring in **December 2027**, and other ruxolitinib patents in **2028**. Third-party analysis puts US generic entry at around **December 2028**. Jakafi is roughly 60% of sales, so the question is whether the pipeline can replace it within about two years.
- **Flattered recent numbers.** Q2 2026 sales rose 40%, but that includes a **$246M one-time Opzelura benefit** from a CMS agreement. Excluding it, growth was 17%. That is still good, but it's the honest figure.
- **A big Q3 charge.** About **$1.27B** of acquired R&D expense from the Vega Therapeutics acquisition will hit Q3 2026 results.
- **Lumpy profits.** The 10-year table shows operating margin swinging from −16% to +29%. Pharma business development (buying drug candidates and taking the charges) makes reported profit uneven, even though the underlying drugs are very profitable.

## Moat and market share
Jakafi demand is still growing (prescriptions up about 9% in Q2 2026). Until generics arrive, its position is protected by patents rather than by competition. After 2028, that moat largely ends for Jakafi in the US.

## What would make this a buy (or not)
- **Bull:** the pipeline and Opzelura replace Jakafi's profits. The current low P/E then means you're paying little for a company with $4B of cash and a growing franchise.
- **Bear:** Jakafi's sales fall sharply after generics arrive. A forward P/E of 13 then becomes much higher on 2029 earnings.

## Check yourself
- The Jakafi revenue bridge to 2029 in the latest 10-K ("Risk Factors" and the patent table).
- Which pipeline products launch before 2028, and their peak-sales estimates.
