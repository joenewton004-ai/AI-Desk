## What it does
Halozyme licenses **ENHANZE**, a technology that turns intravenous drugs into quick under-the-skin injections. Partners pay royalties on sales. The biggest are J&J's **Darzalex Faspro** ($152M of royalties in Q2 2026) and argenx's **Vyvgart Hytrulo** ($73M). Royalties are about 64% of revenue.

## Why the screen likes it
- Revenue is growing about 28% a year (+48% in Q2 2026), with operating margins of 35-60%.
- It is very capital-light: royalties need almost no investment.
- 2026 guidance was raised to $1.835-1.91B in revenue.

## Reading the numbers correctly
- **The EV/EBIT of 32 looks expensive, and the forward P/E of 11 looks cheap.** The gap exists because 2025 profit was cut by a **$285M one-off charge** for the Surf Bio acquisition. Forward earnings are the better guide here.
- **It now carries real debt:** about $2.2B of convertible notes (screen: 3.5× net debt/EBITDA; company-reported about 2.1×). It funded the Elektrofi ($705M) and Surf Bio deals and a $1B buyback programme through 2028.
- **The 10-year table shows the transformation.** It lost money until 2019, then became a highly profitable royalty business once partner drugs scaled.

## The key risk: patent life
- The European ENHANZE patent expires in **March 2029**. One third-party analysis flags pressure on royalties from 2027 onward. Halozyme says its Darzalex SC royalties are secure through 2032. The sources conflict, so this is the single most important thing to verify.
- The Elektrofi (high-concentration injections) and Surf Bio acquisitions are Halozyme's attempt to build a successor platform. It's too early to know whether they will work.
- It is suing Merck over its under-the-skin Keytruda, alleging infringement of its **MDASE** patents. A win would be upside; a loss isn't in the numbers.

## Concentration
More than half of royalties come from two products. A single partner decision, a new competing technology or a patent loss could move the stock sharply.

## Check yourself
- The royalty duration table (by product and region) in the latest 10-K.
- The net-debt and convertible note terms (2027-2032 maturities).
