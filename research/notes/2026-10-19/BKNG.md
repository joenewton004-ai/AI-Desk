## What it does
The world's largest online travel agency: Booking.com, Priceline, Agoda, KAYAK and OpenTable. It earns a commission on hotel and other bookings.

## Why the screen likes it
- Operating margins have been about 27-37% for a decade (excluding the 2020-21 pandemic years).
- FCF margin is about 33%.
- It buys back about **6.6% of its shares a year**.
- It has a forward P/E of about 13.

## About that ROIC
The SEC history shows a pre-tax ROIC of 674% in 2023, and then blank. That's not an error. Customers pay Booking before Booking pays hotels, so the business runs on other people's money and its own invested capital is close to zero or below. That's a genuine strength (a "float"), but the percentage itself is meaningless. Judge it on margins and cash flow instead.

## The 10-year record
Revenue grew from $8.4B to $26.9B. The only bad year was 2020: revenue fell 55%, and operating margin was −9%. Revenue was back above 2019 levels by 2022. Surviving a near-total shutdown of travel and coming back larger is strong evidence of resilience.

## What the market is worried about
- **AI disintermediation.** If people book trips through AI assistants (Google, OpenAI and others), online travel agencies could lose their role as the starting point. Reports describe "AI panic" selloffs, and the shares are down about 27-33% from their high.
- **Slower near-term growth.** Q1 2026 room nights grew 6%, but Q2 guidance was 2-4%. The CFO said the Middle East situation cut about 2 points from growth.

## The case for the defence
- **Scale.** It had about **1.3B room nights** in the last 12 months, with relationships with millions of properties. An AI agent still needs inventory, payments and customer service behind it, and Booking can supply that or become that layer.
- **US momentum.** US domestic room-night growth has accelerated for four straight quarters, to the low teens.

## Check yourself
- The share of bookings coming direct (app and website) vs paid search. Direct traffic is Booking's main defence against intermediaries, including AI.
- Any partnerships with AI assistants, which would show whether Booking becomes the supplier or gets bypassed.
