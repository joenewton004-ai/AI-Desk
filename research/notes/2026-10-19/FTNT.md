## What it does
Cybersecurity: network firewalls (FortiGate), plus secure access for remote users and branches (SASE) and a broad security platform. It designs its own security chips (ASICs), which gives it a price-performance edge over rivals that use off-the-shelf processors.

## The 10-year record
- **Revenue grew from $0.77B to $6.8B, every year**, at 12-32% a year.
- **Operating margin rose steadily from 8% to 31%**, and FCF margin is about 33%.
- **Pre-tax ROIC has been about 60% for five years.**

This is the steadiest record of the ten.

## Why the screen likes it, and why it isn't ranked higher
- **Quality and momentum score near the top.** The stock is up about 104% over 12 months.
- **The value score is near the bottom:** a **forward P/E of about 45 and an EV/EBIT of about 52.** You're paying for excellence.

## What's driving 2026
- **Q2 2026:** revenue $2.05B, **billings +33%**, **product revenue +52%**.
- The combined "SASE firewall" business grew 34% to over $2B, and FortiSASE billings more than doubled.
- Full-year billings guidance is $9.35-9.55B (about +25%).

**The catch:** much of this comes from a **firewall refresh cycle**, as customers replace hardware bought in 2020-2022. Recent price increases also added a low-single-digit boost. Refresh cycles end, and product growth of 40-50% isn't the long-run rate. The 10-year average is about 20%.

## How to think about it
At 45× forward earnings, the price assumes high growth continues after the refresh cycle. A great business isn't always a great investment at any price. This is the kind of name to keep on a watchlist and buy when a cycle slowdown makes it cheaper.

## Check yourself
- Product revenue growth over the next 2-4 quarters. A sharp slowdown would confirm the refresh cycle is ending.
- SASE and services revenue mix. Recurring revenue is what justifies a high multiple.
