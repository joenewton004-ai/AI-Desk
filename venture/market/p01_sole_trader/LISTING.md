## Description

Keep your sole-trader records in one simple spreadsheet and see, all year, roughly how much tax to put aside.

Built for the 2026/27 UK tax year (6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027). Works in Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.

**What's included**
- Start Here guide in plain English
- Income and Expenses logs with dropdown categories that follow HMRC's self-employment expense boxes
- Mileage log using HMRC's simplified rates for 2026/27 (55p a mile for the first 10,000 business miles, then
  25p) - worked out automatically
- Monthly summary (tax months, so the 12 months add up to your tax year)
- Making Tax Digital quarterly view: the four standard update periods and deadlines, with the year-to-date totals
  each update needs
- Tax estimate: Income Tax and Class 4 National Insurance on your profit, and whether the £1,000 trading
  allowance or your actual expenses gives the lower bill
- SA103 box map: which box each total goes in on the short (SA103S) and full (SA103F) self-employment pages
- Built-in checks: example rows left in, dates outside the tax year, expenses with no category
- 10 example rows so you can see how it works, cleared in seconds

**Who it suits**
One-business sole traders in England, Wales or Northern Ireland: freelancers, makers, Etsy sellers, tradespeople,
side hustles.

**What it doesn't do (please read)**
- Scottish Income Tax rates are not included.
- It assumes this business is your only income (no salary, pension, rent or dividends in the estimate).
- It estimates the year's bill only - not payments on account, student loan repayments or VAT.
- It doesn't send anything to HMRC. For Making Tax Digital you still need MTD-compatible or bridging software.

**How delivery works**
This is a digital download - nothing is posted. After payment, Etsy gives you the .xlsx file to download (from your
Purchases page or the email Etsy sends). Open it in Excel, or upload it to Google Drive and choose "Open with
Google Sheets". Rates are on the Settings tab with their GOV.UK sources and the date they were checked.

**Honest notes**
- Built by us with AI assistance; formulas and rules checked by hand. The tax estimate is tested against
  hand-worked examples.
- Not tax advice. This is a record-keeping and estimating tool, not tax or accounting advice. Check GOV.UK or an
  accountant, and you remain responsible for your own tax return.
- Rates checked on GOV.UK on 1 November 2026. If the government changes a 2026/27 rate later, you can update it on
  the Settings tab.
