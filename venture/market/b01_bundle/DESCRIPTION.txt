Two UK spreadsheets that work together for the 2026/27 tax year: price your Etsy items properly, see what you really keep, and keep your sole-trader records ready for Self Assessment. Works in Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.

You get 2 files (18 tabs):

1) UK Etsy Seller Profit Tracker 2026/27
- Price calculator with every UK Etsy fee: 6.5% transaction, 4% + 20p payment processing, the 0.48% regulatory operating fee, listing renewals and Offsite Ads
- VAT on Etsy's fees, with a switch if you give Etsy a VAT number
- Paste your monthly Etsy statement CSV and every line is sorted into sales, refunds and each fee type
- Monthly profit and loss in tax months (6th to 5th)
- HMRC digital-platform reporting counter (30 sales or about £1,700 in a calendar year)

2) UK Sole Trader Tracker 2026/27
- Income, expenses and mileage (HMRC's 2026/27 rate: 55p a mile for the first 10,000 business miles, then 25p)
- Expense categories that follow HMRC's Self Assessment boxes
- Monthly totals and Making Tax Digital quarterly and year-to-date totals
- Income Tax and Class 4 National Insurance estimate, including the £1,000 trading allowance check
- A box map showing where each total goes on your tax return

How they work together
1. Paste your monthly Etsy statement into the Etsy Seller Profit Tracker
2. Copy its monthly totals (already in the Sole Trader Tracker's categories) across
3. See your profit and estimated tax bill all year

What they don't do (please read)
- They don't connect to Etsy or HMRC. You download your Etsy statement and paste it in; for Making Tax Digital you still need MTD-compatible software.
- Tax estimate covers England, Wales and Northern Ireland rates only (not Scottish Income Tax), and assumes this business is your only income.
- Etsy payment accounts in pounds only.

How delivery works
This is a digital download - nothing is posted. After payment, Etsy gives you both .xlsx files to download. Open them in Excel, or upload to Google Drive and choose "Open with Google Sheets".
