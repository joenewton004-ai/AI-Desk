## Description

See what you really keep from every Etsy sale - after every UK fee, VAT on fees and your own costs.

Built for UK Etsy sellers for the 2026/27 tax year (6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027). Works in Microsoft Excel and Google
Sheets.

**What's included**
- Price calculator: type a price, postage and your costs and see each Etsy fee (6.5% transaction, 4% + 20p payment
  processing, the 0.48% regulatory operating fee, the listing renewal, Offsite Ads), VAT on fees, your net profit,
  margin and break-even price
- One switch for VAT: "I give Etsy a VAT number - Yes / No"
- Etsy statement import: paste your monthly statement CSV and every row is sorted into sales, refunds and each fee
  type. Columns are found by their header name, and the sorting rules are editable
- Monthly profit and loss in tax months (6th to 5th), including your own costs (stock, packaging, postage, software)
- HMRC checks: a counter for the digital platform reporting limits (30 sales or about £1,700 in a calendar year) and
  the £1,000 trading allowance
- Monthly totals laid out in the categories of our UK Sole Trader Tracker 2026/27, ready to copy across
- Every fee rate on a Settings tab with its source and the date it was checked - change it if Etsy changes its fees
- Made-up example rows so you can see how it works, cleared in seconds

**What it doesn't do (please read)**
- It doesn't connect to Etsy - you download your monthly statement and paste it in.
- Payment accounts in pounds only. Currency conversion fees aren't worked out.
- It doesn't do VAT returns or work out Income Tax.
- Etsy's statement layout can change. If a row isn't recognised, the sheet marks it "Not sorted - check" and you can
  add a rule.

**How delivery works**
This is a digital download - nothing is posted. After payment, Etsy gives you the .xlsx file to download (from your
Purchases page or the email Etsy sends). Open it in Excel, or upload it to Google Drive and choose "Open with
Google Sheets".

**Honest notes**
- Designed by me; AI tools assisted with parts of the formulas and documentation. The calculations are tested
  against hand-worked examples.
- Not tax or accounting advice. This is a record-keeping and pricing tool. Check GOV.UK, Etsy or an accountant -
  you remain responsible for your own tax return.
- Fees checked on 1 November 2026. Etsy changes its fees from time to time - check its fee page.

Questions? Message me before buying - happy to help.
