# Etsy market research: digital spreadsheet and Power Platform templates (UK seller)

Prepared 2026-11-01. Desk research only: no accounts were created and nobody was contacted.

**Limits of this research. Read these first.**
- The network in this session blocks etsy.com. Etsy's own pages could not be opened directly. Every Etsy fact
  below comes from search-engine excerpts of Etsy's official pages (etsy.com/legal, help.etsy.com, the Seller
  Handbook, community.etsy.com announcements), all accessed 2026-11-01, or from dated third-party reports.
  **Verify every number on Etsy's own fee page and in Shop Manager before paying anything.**
- Listing counts and search volumes come from third-party keyword tools (mainly RankHero). They are estimates,
  and some pages contradict themselves. Treat them as rough direction only.
- Review counts and prices are snapshots of live listings. They change daily, and many listings show permanent
  "40-75% off" sale prices.
- Currency: USD figures are converted at about $1 = £0.76. This is approximate.

### 1.2 Policy on AI-generated and AI-assisted items

- **Disclosure is required.** The Seller Policy says that if an item is created using artificial intelligence, you must
  disclose this in the relevant listings. Etsy's Creativity Standards say to do it in the listing description.
  - [Etsy Seller Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers/)
  - [Etsy Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity)
  - [Etsy Help: What Can I Sell on Etsy?](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360024112614-What-Can-I-Sell-on-Etsy)
- **"Designed by a seller" is the category for AI-assisted work.**
  - Etsy's Creativity Standards (introduced July 2024) list items created with AI tools from the seller's own
    original prompts under "Designed by a seller".
  - Digital files of a seller's original design (graphics, text documents and so on) are also "designed by"
    items.
  - The seller must contribute their own creative input.
  - Sources: [Etsy Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity); [Etsy Seller Handbook: What is Etsy's stance on AI creations?](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1275449912004); [TechCrunch, 2024-07-09](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/09/etsy-new-seller-policy-2024-generative-ai)
- **The rules were tightened on 22 October 2026.** Etsy's announcement "Important updates to AI on Etsy" did four
  things:
  - It banned AI-generated "functional guides": patterns, recipes and similar instructions.
  - It barred bot-made listings with no human involvement.
  - It introduced new Listing Image Requirements. Most listings need one unedited photo of the real item. For
    **digital downloads, the rule is instead a visual preview or excerpt of the content the buyer receives.**
  - AI art stays allowed with meaningful creative direction and disclosure.
  - Sources: [Etsy Community: Important updates to AI on Etsy](https://community.etsy.com/forum/announcements-290/topic/important-updates-to-ai-on-etsy-190573/); [Etsy Listing Image Requirements](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/listing-image-requirements/253962679005); [Etsy Help: How Listing Image Requirements apply](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/44297885802647-How-Do-Etsy-s-Listing-Image-Requirements-Apply-to-My-Listings); [Digital Commerce 360, 2026-10-26](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/10/26/etsy-ai-policy/); [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-bans-ai-patterns/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/etsy-forces-sellers-to-show-one-unaltered-photo-in-every-listing/)
  - **Not settled:** the announcement does not say whether a spreadsheet's how-to guide counts as a "functional
    guide", and no effective date or grace period was given. **Keep instruction guides human-written.**
- **The originality rule (June 2025), as reported by third parties.** Etsy reportedly removed the allowance for
  items made "using a templated design or pattern". Items must now be "based on a seller's original design".
  Reselling bought or PLR templates, or lightly edited Canva or stock templates, is a removal risk.
  - Sources: [RankHero blog, 2025-06-20](https://blog.rankhero.com/2025/06/20/etsy-policy-changes/); [Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/etsy-creativity-standards)
- **Enforcement.** Etsy can remove listings, and repeated or large-scale breaches can lead to shop suspension.
  [Etsy Help: Reasons a listing may be removed under the Creativity Standards](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/34707360607511-Reasons-a-Listing-May-Be-Removed-Under-Etsy-s-Creativity-Standards)

**What this means for us (conservative reading):**
- Build every workbook ourselves.
- If AI helped create the product itself (formulas, layout, guide text), add a plain line to the description, for
  example: *"Designed by me; AI tools assisted with parts of the formulas and documentation, all tested by
  hand."*
- Use real screenshots of the actual sheets for the preview images. Don't use AI mockups that show features the
  file doesn't have.
- Never auto-generate listings in bulk without human review.

### 1.3 Digital downloads

- Allowed when the content is your own.
- Two types:
  - **Instant download:** files are available straight after payment.
  - **Made-to-order:** you upload the file later through the Orders page.
- Up to **5 files per listing, 20 MB each**.
- Buyers can review a digital item for 100 days from first download, or 12 months from purchase if sooner.
- Digital-only shops can still earn Star Seller.
- Not allowed: AI prompt bundles, and books or PDFs the seller did not create.
- Sources: [Etsy Seller Handbook: How to Sell Digital Downloads](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/47330319230); [Etsy Help: How to Manage Your Digital Listings](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628347-How-to-Manage-Your-Digital-Listings); [Etsy Help: What Can I Sell on Etsy?](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-in/articles/360024112614-What-Can-I-Sell-on-Etsy)

### 1.4 UK tax context

**HMRC platform reporting**
- Etsy reports sellers to HMRC unless they make fewer than 30 sales **and** receive €2,000 (about £1,700) or less
  in a calendar year.
- Income above the **£1,000 trading allowance** must be reported to HMRC.
- Sources: [GOV.UK: Check if you need to tell HMRC about income from online platforms](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/check-if-you-need-to-tell-hmrc-about-your-income-from-online-platforms); [GOV.UK: Selling goods or services on a digital platform](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/selling-goods-or-services-on-a-digital-platform)

**Making Tax Digital for Income Tax.** This is a selling point for product 1 below.
- From April 2026, it is compulsory for sole traders and landlords with qualifying income over £50,000.
- The threshold falls to £30,000 from April 2027 and £20,000 from April 2028.
- Spreadsheets can still be used, but only when linked to **bridging software**.
- Sources: [GOV.UK: MTD for Income Tax collection](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/making-tax-digital-for-income-tax); [GOV.UK: Use MTD for Income Tax, before you use this guide](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/use-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax/before-you-use-this-guide); [GOV.UK: Reduction of threshold to £20,000 from April 2028](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/making-tax-digital-for-income-tax-self-assessment-reducing-the-mandation-threshold-from-30000-to-20000-from-april-2028/reduction-of-the-mandation-threshold-from-30000-to-20000-from-april-2028)

## 3. Recommended first three products

The common thread: **UK small-business money admin, built by someone who actually builds data tools.** It plays
to the owner's skills and to the UK-only angle that US-built templates miss. It also reuses one data model across
all three products, so each one is cheaper to make.

### Product 1: UK sole trader bookkeeping and Self Assessment tracker (Excel + Google Sheets), tax year 2026/27

**Price:** £14.99. Launch at £11.99 for the first 2-3 weeks to collect reviews. Don't go below £9.

**What's in it:**
- Income and expense log using **HMRC SA103S/SA103F expense categories**, mapped box by box.
- **Mileage at HMRC simplified rates** (45p/25p).
- Use-of-home flat rates.
- A **£1,000 trading allowance vs actual expenses** comparison.
- A rough tax and Class 4 NI estimate, clearly labelled as an estimate.
- Payments-on-account reminders.
- **Quarterly summaries laid out for MTD** (6 Apr-5 Jul and so on). The guide explains that MTD needs bridging
  software. **Do not claim the product is "HMRC-approved".**

**Why:**
- A real UK search corner with dedicated Etsy UK search pages.
- An annual repurchase cycle (a new tax year each April).
- MTD brings about 970,000 more people into scope by 2028, which creates fresh demand.
- Prices of £2-£30 show buyers will pay into the teens for a polished version.

**How to beat the top sellers:**
1. Locked formula cells and data validation, so buyers can't break it.
2. A "check" tab that flags uncategorised or duplicate rows.
3. Works properly in **both** Excel and Google Sheets. Many listings are really one or the other.
4. A dated "rules used" box (rates, thresholds, tax year) with GOV.UK links, plus a free update for the next tax
   year.
5. A short screen-recorded walkthrough video.

**Risk:** if the tax content is wrong, we get bad reviews and refund requests. Add a clear "not tax advice; check
with HMRC or an accountant" line, and re-check every rate against GOV.UK before each tax year.

### Product 2: UK Etsy and online-seller profit tracker with Etsy CSV import (Google Sheets + Excel)

**Price:** £12.99. Bundle with Product 1 at £22.

**What's in it:**
- Paste in Etsy's monthly CSV, and it splits out every UK fee automatically: 6.5% transaction, 4% + £0.20
  processing, the **0.48% Regulatory Operating Fee**, listing and renewal fees, Offsite Ads, and **VAT on fees for
  non-VAT-registered sellers**.
- True profit per product.
- A tracker for the **HMRC platform-reporting threshold (30 sales / about £1,700)**.
- A £1,000 trading-allowance meter.
- An export tab that feeds Product 1.

**Why:**
- Etsy-seller bookkeeping listings are among the most-reviewed in the category (about 9-10.8k reviews), so demand
  is proven.
- Existing ones are US-centric, built around US sales tax and Printify. A UK-fee-correct version is a clear,
  explainable difference.
- The buyers are Etsy sellers, so they are already on Etsy.

**How to beat the top sellers:**
- Be correct for UK fees and VAT (most aren't).
- Automatic CSV mapping with a "paste here" tab.
- Charts on a single dashboard tab.

**Example evidence:**
- [Etsy Seller Bookkeeping Spreadsheet](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1762253018/etsy-seller-bookkeeping-spreadsheet-etsy)
- [POD variant](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1657487524/etsy-seller-bookkeeping-spreadsheet-pod)

### Product 3: Small-business finance dashboard for Power BI (.pbit) that reads the Product 1 workbook

**Price:** £24.99 alone, or £34.99 in an "Excel + Power BI" bundle with Product 1.

**What's in it:**
- A Power BI template file that connects to the Product 1 Excel file.
- Monthly profit and loss.
- Expense categories vs the previous year.
- A tax-set-aside gauge.
- Cash runway.
- A 5-minute setup video. Power BI Desktop is free.

**Why:**
- This is the owner's genuine edge.
- Power BI listings sell at $9-$33. Most are generic dashboards on sample data that buyers have to re-wire to
  their own data.
- A dashboard that **works on day one with a workbook the buyer already uses** is rare.
- It raises the average order value and makes the shop look professional.

**Honest caveat:** the Power BI buyer pool on Etsy is small, and there is no search-volume data. Expect this
product to sell less than Products 1-2. It is also the best item to cross-list on Gumroad or Payhip and promote
on LinkedIn.

### Not first: why the other niches were left out

- **Wedding planners:** big demand, but incumbents with thousands of sales and design-led listings.
- **Generic budget planners:** saturated, with search falling about 33-48%.
- **Power Apps / Power Automate:** almost no Etsy buyers.
- **Project trackers:** a reasonable fourth product once the shop has reviews, ideally with a SharePoint or
  Microsoft Lists version.

## 4. Listing best practices for digital templates (2026)

### Titles
- Etsy updated its title guidance in April 2026. A third-party report gives the limit as **under about 15 words**
  (the field allows 140 characters).
- Put the main buyer phrase first, then the platform and audience.
- Example: *"UK Sole Trader Bookkeeping Spreadsheet 2026/27, Self Assessment Tax Tracker, Excel & Google Sheets"*.
- Etsy search now weighs the title, tags, attributes, description, first photo and reviews together.
- Sources:
  - [CraftGlowAI: Etsy listing titles 2026](https://www.craftglowai.com/blog/etsy-listing-titles-2026)
  - [InsightAgent: Etsy search algorithm 2026](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/etsy-search-algorithm-updates-2026)
  - [Listify: Etsy SEO guide 2026](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-seo-guide-2026)

### 13 tags
- Each tag can be **up to 20 characters**.
- Use every slot, use multi-word phrases, and don't waste tags repeating the title.
- No punctuation.
- Include the platform ("google sheets", "excel template") and UK terms ("self assessment", "hmrc",
  "sole trader uk").
- Source: [Artomate: Etsy titles and tags 2026](https://www.artomate.app/blog/how-to-write-etsy-titles-and-tags-2026)

### Photos and video
- Etsy allows up to **20 images plus a video**. The limit was 10 before the October 2026 update. Use at least
  1000px; 2000px is recommended.
- Images must be a **real preview of the content**. That is now a policy requirement for digital downloads
  (section 1.2).
- Suggested image order:
  1. Hero image: an actual screenshot on a laptop frame, plus a short headline.
  2. "What's included" list of tabs.
  3. Each key tab, readable when zoomed.
  4. Excel + Sheets compatibility badge.
  5. "How you receive it" (PDF > link > Make a copy).
  6. UK-specific features.
  7. A real use example with dummy data.
- Add a 5-15 second silent screen recording of data being typed in and the dashboard updating.
- Sources:
  - [MyDesigns: optimise digital listings](https://mydesigns.io/blog/how-to-optimize-your-etsy-listings-to-sell-more-digital-products/)
  - [Elistit: Etsy SEO for digital sellers](https://elistit.com/guides/etsy-seo/)
  - [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-bans-ai-patterns/)

### Delivery

**Google Sheets**
- Upload a PDF containing a **"/copy" link**: replace `/edit...` in the share URL with `/copy`. Opening the link
  makes the buyer their own copy.
- Share the master file as **Viewer** (one blog says Editor; don't, because buyers could change the master).
- Test the link in an incognito window.

## 7. Biggest risks (honest)

1. **Nobody finds the listings.** The category is crowded, prices race to the bottom (permanent 40-75% "sales",
   £1-£5 prices), and search volume for generic spreadsheet terms fell 33-48% over the last year. A new shop with
   zero reviews may sell nothing for weeks. The UK-specific niche is the mitigation, not a guarantee.
2. **Tax accuracy and liability.** HMRC rates and MTD rules change, and one wrong figure brings bad reviews.
   Re-check every rate against GOV.UK each tax year, date the rules box, and include a "not tax advice"
   disclaimer.
3. **Etsy policy drift.** Originality (June 2025) and AI rules (October 2026) tightened with little notice. Mitigation:
   only original builds, honest AI disclosure, and real-screenshot previews.
4. **Copying.** Spreadsheets are easy to resell. Watermark the guide PDF, and don't expose anything we wouldn't
   want copied.
