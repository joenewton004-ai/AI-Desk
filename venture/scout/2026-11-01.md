## 1. Rule changes that affect our live modules

### 1a. Etsy - AI rules update of 22 Oct 2026 (HIGH relevance to Market)
What the coverage says (several secondary sources agree; I could not read Etsy's own post):
- **AI-generated patterns, recipes and instructions are banned**: knitting/sewing patterns, woodworking plans and
  "similar guides meant to help buyers complete a specific task". The seller must write that content themselves, and
  the listing must show a real result made by following it.
- **Bot-generated listings with no human involvement are banned.** So is AI use that misleads buyers.
- **AI-made digital downloads are still allowed** (planners, journals, ebooks, art), provided the seller has "a clear
  creative role" and **discloses AI use**. Digital downloads need a visual preview or excerpt of what the buyer gets.
- **Listing Image Requirements were refreshed the same day**: at least one unaltered photo of the real item. Coverage
  says this is aimed at physical items, with exceptions for digital downloads/POD. The exact scope for downloads is unclear.

**What it means for us:**
1. Spreadsheet and Power Platform templates are digital downloads, so they should still be allowed with AI
   disclosure. But **a "how to use / how to build" PDF bundled with a template could count as "instructions"**.
   Rule for Market: the owner writes or genuinely rewrites any instruction guide, and the listing previews are real
   screenshots of the actual template (no AI mock-ups that look different from the file).
2. The owner must review and post every listing personally. Claude drafts, a human publishes. Our PLAN already works
   this way, so make it an explicit rule.
3. Tick Etsy's AI-use disclosure on every listing (already in PLAN ground rules).
- **Check before listing:** Etsy Seller Handbook post of 22 Oct 2026, Creativity Standards, Listing Image Requirements.
  Pass this section to the Etsy research agent.

### 1b. YouTube - "inauthentic content" (MEDIUM relevance to Studio)
- On 15 Jul 2025 the "repetitious content" monetisation policy was renamed "inauthentic content". YouTube called it a
  minor update with no new policy. It targets mass-produced, template-like videos with little variation, AI-made or not.
  I found **no confirmed new October 2026 rule**. One headline (medianews4u, ~23 Oct 2026, "AI-driven visuals, voices
  and deepfakes barred from YouTube monetization") couldn't be opened, and nothing else confirms it.
- SEO blogs report heavy 2026 enforcement against faceless AI channels (e.g. "4.7 billion views wiped in January
  2026"). That number is unverified.
- One unverified guide claims channels using **AI personas to give financial advice** can't be monetised.
- **What it means for us:** Studio is in decent shape. Each episode is a different test with real data, the voice is
  the owner's own, and every episode is framed as education. The risk is **Shorts that all look the same** (same
  template, same hook format). Vary their structure, and keep the owner's real voice rather than TTS. Tick the
  altered/synthetic label only where realistic AI visuals are used. Never use an AI presenter persona.
- **Check:** YouTube Help pages for "YouTube channel monetization policies" (inauthentic content section) and "Disclosing
  altered or synthetic content". Also confirm whether the medianews4u story points to a real policy change.

### 1c. UK - FCA "finfluencer" enforcement (MEDIUM relevance to Studio, and to any paid trading product)
- FCA-led international week of action from 20 Apr 2026: 120 account takedown requests and 1,267 illegal financial ads
  identified. Seven influencers were sentenced at Southwark Crown Court over an unauthorised FX scheme, more trials
  are listed for Feb/Mar 2027, and about 20 finfluencers are reported to be under caution. The FCA's social media
  guidance dates from March 2024.
- **What it means for us:** our "we tested it and it didn't work" content isn't promoting a financial product. The
  risk comes from **affiliate links to brokers, trading apps, prop firms or crypto** (a financial promotion if
  unapproved), and from anything that reads like a personal recommendation. Rule: **no broker/trading-app affiliate
  links** in Studio without a separate check. Keep the disclaimer. Never say "buy X".
- **Check:** FCA "Finfluencers" guidance (FG24/1) and the FCA perimeter guidance before any paid product about trading.

### 1d. UK tax / HMRC (LOW now, keep on the radar)
- **Platform reporting:** Etsy (and YouTube/Google as a service platform) reports a seller to HMRC each year unless
  the seller has **both fewer than 30 sales and under about €2,000 (≈£1,700)** in the calendar year. Data is sent by
  31 January for the previous year. The rules started on 1 Jan 2024. If Market hits its targets we would likely cross
  30 sales in 2027, so expect to be reported. That's fine if the income is declared.
- **Trading allowance:** £1,000 gross a tax year. Above that, the owner registers for Self Assessment. (I didn't
  confirm this in today's search; check gov.uk.) Venture income counts as the owner's (the JV isn't a company).
- **Making Tax Digital for Income Tax:** started 6 Apr 2026 for qualifying income over £50k, falling to £30k (Apr 2027)
  and £20k (Apr 2028). Irrelevant at our scale.
- **YouTube/AdSense:** the owner should submit US tax info (W-8BEN, UK treaty) when monetising. Without it Google
  withholds US tax on US-viewer earnings. Check the current AdSense tax help page.
- Keep `ledger.csv` as the record; it already matches what HMRC would ask for.
- Sources: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/selling-goods-or-services-on-a-digital-platform ;

  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/making-tax-digital ; https://www.hazells.co.uk/making-tax-digital/

## 2. Threats in our niches

### 2a. Spreadsheet templates: Excel's own AI builds workbooks from a prompt
- Microsoft made Agent Mode in Excel generally available on 27 Jan 2026 for commercial Copilot and for **Microsoft 365
  Personal/Family** subscribers. It builds tables, formulas, PivotTables and charts from one prompt (e.g. "build a
  quarterly revenue forecast with a summary dashboard"). In January it was **not yet available in the UK/EU**.
  Secondary reports say it became the default Copilot mode in Word/Excel/PowerPoint on 22 Apr 2026 (unverified).
- **Impact:** simple budget/tracker templates get commoditised, mainly in the US, which is most of Etsy's demand.
  Templates that keep their value are ones that are opinionated (a worked system, a good design, a real process the
  buyer doesn't know how to specify) or that target Google Sheets users. Flag to the Etsy research agent. This is a
  slow threat, not a reason to stop.
- Sources: https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/excelblog/agent-mode-in-excel-is-now-generally-available-on-desktop/4457408 ;
  https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/1401/microsoft-copilot-agent-mode-word-excel-powerpoint-april-2026

### 2b. Power Platform templates
- Microsoft's Power Platform catalog (GA April 2024) and template gallery give away templates free. A third-party paid
  library exists (powerapps-template.com, "pay once" plus free options). I found **no evidence of meaningful paid
  demand** on Gumroad/Etsy. It's a small niche, and buyers are businesses that also have Microsoft's free templates.
- Sources: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/release-plan/2024wave1/data-platform/make-building-easier-power-platform-catalog ;
  https://www.producthunt.com/products/power-apps-templates ; https://alternativeto.net/software/powerapps-template/about

### 2c. "We backtested it" trading videos
- The niche is established. **Trading Strategy Testing** (about 845k subscribers per a vidIQ listing, data July 2025),
  **Trading Rush** (long-running backtest channel) and **Quantified Strategies** (blog + Substack with paid-only posts
  and a "109 backtested strategies" playlist) all operate in it. On r/algotrading (Feb 2026), "I backtested a 400K views
  YouTube trading strategy (the results were BRUTAL)" did well. People want debunks.
- **What sets us apart:** most competitors test "100 trades by hand" or quote win rates with no costs. Ours use 10
  years of data, include costs, test random placebos and say "survivors-only". That only works as a selling point if
  the videos say it in the first 30 seconds. **Competitors are proof of demand, not a reason to stop.** Shorts pay very
  little (finance Shorts ≈ £0.15-0.40 per 1,000 views, unverified SEO estimates; long-form UK finance RPM ≈ £6-18).
  Studio's money is in long-form plus a product, not Shorts.

## 3. Ideas beyond Etsy + YouTube

### Idea A - Freelance "honest backtest" service (Fiverr / Upwork)  - score 4/5
- **What:** the buyer sends their strategy rules. We deliver a fixed-format report: costs included, no-lookahead check,
  random-entry placebo, split-half test, and a plain-English verdict. It reuses the repo's backtest/validate/walkforward
  tooling and the research write-up format.
- **Startup cost:** £0 (Fiverr takes 20% of each order; Upwork has its own fees and paid "connects", optional).
- **Time to first £:** about 2-4 weeks once the gig is live (new gigs rank poorly; one buyer review is the hurdle).
- **Evidence:** at least 20 live Fiverr gigs selling exactly this ("backtest your trading strategy in python", "convert
  your TradingView indicator into a backtested strategy", "audit your Pine Script for repainting/look-ahead"). One
  seller shows 101 completed orders at 4.8 stars. Search accessed 2026-11-01, gig pages not opened, **no prices seen**.
  Several gigs promise profits. Our way in is the opposite: "most strategies fail, here's the evidence".
  Examples: https://fiverr.com/maithili_d_/backtest-your-trading-strategies-in-python ;
  https://fiverr.com/rickhardypro/audit-and-review-your-tradingview-pine-script-indicator-or-strategy ;
  https://fiverr.com/omerkhan99/backtest-your-trading-strategy-with-statistical-analysis-and-real-data
- **Fit:** the best of anything found. The research/ folder is effectively a portfolio of 40+ worked examples, and
  Studio videos become the gig's proof (and vice versa).
- **Risks:** owner time per order (Claude does the analysis, but the owner holds the account and talks to buyers);
  crowded and price-competitive; buyers wanting "optimise until it wins" (refuse curve-fitting, which costs sales);
  data licensing (deliver results, never resell raw Alpaca/Yahoo data); FCA (testing a client's own rules is research,
  not a personal recommendation, but **never say "trade this"**, and check FG24/1 / perimeter guidance); disputes and
  refunds on a 20%-fee platform.
- **First test (cheap, owner's call):** Claude writes the gig text, 3 package tiers and a sample report built from an
  existing write-up (e.g. ICT Silver Bullet). Before opening anything, the owner spends 20 minutes checking the prices
  of the top 10 Fiverr gigs. If the owner agrees, they open a Fiverr seller account (free) and publish one gig.
- **Kill rule:** fewer than 2 paid orders within 45 days of the gig going live, OR average owner time over 1.5 hours an
  order at the price we can charge (below about £15/hour net), so stop.
